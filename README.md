## Abstract

The Unified Physical Correspondence (UPC) is the operator-based projection of the Monistic Continuum Model (MCM) onto quantum mechanics (QM) and general relativity (GR).  
It introduces a discrete vortex basis and two functorial projections:  
- a spectral projection for quantum behavior,  
- and a topological projection for relativistic behavior.

Both projections arise from a single operator algebra consisting of geometric, topological, dynamical, stability, and clustering operators.  
This unified structure ensures mathematical closure and ontological consistency.

Quantum nonlocality and relativistic locality are interpreted as complementary manifestations of the same underlying continuum architecture.  
The theory establishes axiomatic completeness and internal consistency through a stability condition that guarantees invariant behavior of stable vortex configurations.

UPC provides a unified operator framework for transitions between discrete and continuous physical regimes.

## Core Concepts

- **Monistic Medium**  
  A single non-mechanical medium without fundamental space, time, fields, or particles.

- **Discrete Vortex Basis**  
  A set of vortex cells carrying geometric, topological, and dynamical parameters.

- **Operator Algebra**  
  A unified set of operators generating geometry, topology, dynamics, stability, and clustering.

- **Functorial Projections**  
  Spectral projection → quantum fields  
  Topological projection → curvature and relativistic structure

- **Stability Condition**  
  Defines when vortex configurations behave as stable physical structures.
